Shubh Deepavali

A night of lamps, rockets and gold. Tap the sky to launch one.

Enter the festival

Five nights of light

  1. 1

    Dhanteras

    Homes are cleaned, new metal is bought for luck, and the first lamps are lit.

  2. 2

    Naraka Chaturdashi

    Before dawn, oil baths and a row of lamps mark the end of darkness.

  3. 3

    Lakshmi Puja

    The main night. Every doorway glows while families pray for prosperity.

  4. 4

    Govardhan Puja

    Mountains of food are offered in gratitude, and many regions begin a new year.

  5. 5

    Bhai Dooj

    Siblings meet, exchange gifts and bless one another.

Rituals of the night

  • Diyas

    Clay lamps filled with ghee or oil, lit at dusk along doorways and windows.

  • Rangoli

    Patterns of coloured powder and petals drawn at the entrance to welcome guests.

  • Puja

    Prayers to Lakshmi and Ganesha with flowers, sweets and incense.

  • Mithai

    Laddoos, barfi and jalebi shared with neighbours and family.
